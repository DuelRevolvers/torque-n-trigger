Notes for moving these into the game

  • Car body textures are greyscale on purpose. They multiply the paint colour, so the garage paint picker keeps working. The rust sheet is the exception: it is in colour and replaces the paint look on Junk parts.
  • The car shell has no UVs yet. extrudeProfile in carView.js would need the planar UVs this page generates (sides projected in metres, windows fitted to 0..1).
  • Road, barrier and building sheets keep the current sizes and layouts (128x256 road with the same lane lines, 64x32 barrier, 64x128 facade with 4 px window cells), so the track and city UVs don't change.
  • The road adds a roughness map so puddles and tyre tracks look glossier than the dry asphalt. Use it with roughness: 1 on the road material.
  • This preview skips the game's dither, scanlines and rain. Bloom and vertex snap are the same as in the game.
