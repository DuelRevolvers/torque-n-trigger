# Handoff: Torque & Trigger HUD restyle

## Goal
Restyle the in-game HUD of Torque & Trigger to match `concept art/T&T HUD Concept Art 02.jpg`, a component sheet. The ammo icons for the equipped weapons come from `concept art/Ammo Icons Concept Art 02 No Special.jpg`. The user's follow-up asked for two more things:
- indicators showing how many boosts you have
- a fix for the BOOST label overlapping the speedometer

## Done so far
- **`src/ui/hud.js` rewritten.** The new `drawPane` is drawn in 270-line layout units, and a new `dial()` method caches the static speedometer face.
  - Riveted metal plate with a segmented HEALTH bar.
  - AMMO box: 7-segment LED digits with a ghosted "888" behind them, the primary weapon's icon in a well, and a segmented HEAT bar.
  - SEC box: secondary weapon icon plus a 2-digit LED count.
  - Utility box (OIL / SMOKE / SHIELD / REPAIR): shows READY or the cooldown seconds.
  - Ammo states: red when ammo is ≤15% of capacity or empty, flashing while reloading. When overheated the heat bar goes red and a ⚠ triangle flashes.
  - Speedometer: chrome ring, red zone at 80% and up, orange needle, LED readout plus MPH or KM/H, and the gear number in the dial. Radius is 40 on desktop and 26 on touch; desktop centre is `gx = W - R - 40`.
  - Boost: a capsule of cyan segments over a red reserve cell. Beside it is a column of charge cells, one per charge (2–4). Cyan means ready, flashing white means burning, dark teal filling up means recharging, dark means used.
  - Position and lap are big outlined numbers at top-left. The timer and event title are at top-right.
  - Lock-on reticle: corner brackets around a ringed crosshair, centred at marker y+16.
  - Removed: the weapon name text and the rev ring.
- **`src/ui/ammo-icons.png` (new).** A 576x64 sheet of nine 64px cells in this order: chaingun, scatter, plasma, flamethrower, railgun, turret, mines, rockets, tesla. These names match the weapon keys in `src/parts/catalog.js`.
  - It's loaded with `new URL('./ammo-icons.png', import.meta.url)`, guarded by `typeof Image`.
  - The icons were cut from the concept JPG by a throwaway PowerShell/C# script in the session scratchpad. That script is not in the repo.
  - Icons are drawn at full canvas resolution, cached per type and size.
