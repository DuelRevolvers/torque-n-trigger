# Session handoff

Written 10/4/2026, 9:06:30 PM at 222k of context.

## Goal
Two features for the T&T SDK, a map editor for the Torque & Trigger game (`sdk.html`, `src/sdk/`):
1. A **Customize Car** button immediately left of **▶ Test drive**. It opens an editor that looks like the custom-car showroom in `texture-lab.html`. Test drives then use that car, and the editor has an option to go back to a random test-drive car.
2. Bots that can be added from the test-drive pause menu. The test drive runs in the game tab the SDK opens, `index.html?testdrive`.

## Done so far
- `src/parts/customCar.js` (new):
  - `TEST_CAR = 'testcar'` is the storage key, holding `{ use, design }`.
  - `randomDesign()` uses `randomBuild`, so random cars fit their chassis.
  - `customBuild(design)` makes one quality per car or `'mixed'`, with traits seeded per slot.
- `src/sdk/carEditor.js` (new):
  - A modal `#car-editor` with its own WebGLRenderer, rendered at 360 lines with bloom. It holds the Texture Lab showroom scene: CarView on a turntable.
  - Controls: Quality, paint swatches plus a colour picker, light swatches, and a select per slot.
  - Buttons: Random / Fill all slots / Bare / Whole car / Spin.
  - A toggle: "Test drive in: This car / A random car". Editing any part sets "This car".
  - Saves on every change via `sdkPut`. Esc, a click outside, or Done closes it. While it's open, SDK keys are blocked by a capture listener.
- `src/sdk/page.js`: the `#customize-car` button and the `#car-editor` CSS.
- `src/sdk/main.js`:
  - Imports the editor and wires the button.
  - `frame()` skips the SDK render while the editor is open.
  - Controls panel entries added to `FIXED_KEYS` (Esc) and `MOUSE` (drag, wheel, click outside).
- `src/main.js`:
  - `testDrive()` uses `customBuild` when `sdkGet(TEST_CAR).use` is set.
  - Pause actions `ADD BOT (n/7)` and `REMOVE BOTS`, only in free-roam test drives (`roaming()`, `MAX_BOTS = 7`).
- `src/ui/settingsMenu.js`: an action `label` may now be a function.
- `src/screens/raceScreen.js`:
  - `get bots()` and `addBot()`, which makes a random DRIVERS entry at `this.tier`, arena AI included.
  - `botPose()` places a bot on the nearest `track.def.roadPoints` crossroads at least 35 m off and at least 12 m from other cars, facing the player.
  - `removeBots()` also drops the removed bots' projectiles and mines.
- `docs/tt-sdk-design.md`: §7.10, the §12 Fixed row, the §13 "Top bar" row, and a new "Customize Car and bots" row.
- `.claude/launch.json` (new): a "vite" config on port 5199.
