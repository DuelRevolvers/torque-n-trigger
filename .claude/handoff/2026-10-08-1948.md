## Done so far
- **Drivable tops:** `fitsCar(o, ground)` is exported from `src/sim/track.js`.
  - A top counts as ground when its footprint is at least 2.4 m × 4.6 m (`FIT_W`, `FIT_L`) and it stands at least 1 m (`MIN_RISE`) above the ground under it.
  - `ground` can be a function, which is called only when the footprint fits.
  - Street races: `Track.isTop(o, hint)` replaces `o.top` in `standY`, `clearIndex` and `solidAt`. Results are cached in the `this.fits` WeakMap, set in the constructor. An earlier version worked this out for every obstacle at build time, which made the test suite much slower; the lazy check fixed that.
  - `track.tops`, which controls spawning on top of things, is still based only on the editor's `top` flags.
  - Arenas and free roam: `src/sim/arena.js` imports `fitsCar` and `onFoot`. The constructor builds the `this.drivable` Set. In `query`, a car whose height is at least the top minus 0.1 stands on the top instead of hitting a wall.
- **Barriers:** the side walls of races and arenas already stopped cars at any height. Sprints and drag strips had no barrier at their open ends.
  - `END_RUN = 20` in `track.js`.
  - The `Track.ends` getter returns `{ k, ox, oz, x, z, y, wall }` for each open end.
  - `Track.endCap(g, x, z)` makes everything 20 m past each open end a wall. `query()` applies it only when the nearest sample is the first or last one and the car isn't on a shortcut branch.
  - `src/render/trackView.js`: `endPens(track, material)` draws a 1.1 m barrier along both sides and across the road at each open end.
- **Controller menus (`src/ui/padNav.js`):**
  - Fixed the press check: `edge` read `this.prev` after it had been overwritten, so A, B, LB and RB never fired.
  - B also finds buttons whose text starts with `BACK`.
  - A on a `<select>` opens it as an in-place list (`size` attribute, `pad-open` class). Up or down picks, skipping disabled options; A keeps the pick and dispatches `change`; B restores the old value.
  - Left and right move `input[type=range]` sliders.
  - `index.html` adds `.pad-open { outline-color: #4de1ff !important; }`.
- **Tests:**
  - New `tests/tops.test.js` has 5 tests.
  - `tests/events.test.js`: the drag test now checks the highest gear each car reached, because cars now stop at the end barrier and shift down.
